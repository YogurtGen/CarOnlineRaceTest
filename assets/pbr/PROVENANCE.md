Each material uses original 1K JPEG diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness maps. Diffuse images use sRGB; normal/roughness are data textures. The environment uses the original 1K Radiance HDR image for reflection and background. Local filenames changed for brevity; source pixels are unmodified. UV scale, material tint and normal strength are configured at runtime.

Author names and source credit appear in-game and in THIRD_PARTY.md, although CC0 does not require attribution.
